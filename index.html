Fair questions, straight answers.

Why wouldn't I just get my own Claude subscription?

You could. The subscription is the cheap part. What you're buying is the setup around it: the site it can edit, the product list, the brand guide, the templates and the rules. That took me two years to get right at Parkbus. You get it in two weeks.

Will it work with my booking system?

Almost certainly. FareHarbor, Checkfront, Rezdy, Peek, whatever you use. We link to it; we don't replace it.

Do I need to be technical?

No. You need to be able to type what you want in a sentence. Your Scout shows you the change before it publishes, and you have 30 days of me to call on.

Why would a bus company guy do this?

Because I'm a tourism operator first. I know what a Saturday in July feels like. I built this for my own business before offering it to yours.
