You could. The subscription is the cheap part. What you're buying is the setup around it: the site it can edit, the product list, the brand guide, the templates and the rules. That took me two years to get right at Parkbus. You get it in two weeks.

Almost certainly. FareHarbor, Checkfront, Rezdy, Peek, whatever you use. We link to it; we don't replace it.
